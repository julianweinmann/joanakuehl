Everything you might want to know before your first session

In our first session, we get to know each other in a safe and confidential space. You tell me what's on your mind, and together we explore whether and how I can support you. There's no pressure — the session is about finding a good connection. The initial session lasts about 50 minutes and can take place online or at my practice in Berlin.

Psychological counseling supports people facing difficult life situations, decisions, or personal challenges. Psychotherapy goes deeper and addresses mental health conditions such as depression, anxiety disorders, or trauma. As a psychotherapist in training, I offer both — we'll find the right framework together in our first session.

Both. I offer sessions at my practice in Berlin as well as online video sessions. Online sessions are equally effective and give you maximum flexibility — especially if you live outside Berlin or have a busy schedule.

Yes. Multiple scientific meta-analyses show that video-based psychotherapy achieves comparable outcomes to in-person sessions. A 2021 meta-analysis confirms that the difference between video and in-person therapy is negligible. Specifically for systemic therapy, a recent study (2025) also found equivalent results. What matters most is the therapeutic relationship — and that can develop beautifully online, too.

High sensitivity (HSP) affects about 15–20% of the population and can show up as intense sensory perception, deep empathy, quick overstimulation, or a strong need for solitude. There are various online self-tests that can offer a first orientation. In my experience, though, high sensitivity is best understood together in conversation — because everyone experiences it differently.
